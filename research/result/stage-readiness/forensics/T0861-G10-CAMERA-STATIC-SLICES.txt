Key structural facts:
* the values compared against the arguments are built from TWO persistent
  sources: the field-context member `[fieldctx+0xD4]` (slots 4/5) and the
  scene member `[scene+0x1CC]` (same slots), plus the scene world bounds
  `[scene+0x1D0]/[scene+0x1D4]` (the VR-fold family, C-026/C-084).
* `[fieldctx+0xD4]` is NOT a transient caller value — 0x48AEC3 is a plain
  member getter (`out=[this+0xD4]`, conditional AddRef, ret 4). The T086
  C-101 wording "the only persistent camera fields are the load-time VR fold
  bounds ... the per-frame camera value is caller-transient by construction"
  is therefore OVERSTATED for the slot15 neighborhood: persistent state is
  READ here through an indirection whose holder is a field-context member.
