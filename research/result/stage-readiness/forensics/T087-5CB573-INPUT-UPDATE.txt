## 6. Coordinate-surface conclusion
The singleton position (+0x938/+0x93C) lives in SCREEN space (init at the
centre, hard screen clamp, cursor sprites, UI-grid producers 0x4BEDE7 /
0x5F1C7D, WM-mouse forwarding). The WORLD camera lives in the shared
Shape2D#Vector2D ([fieldctx+0xD4] == [singleton+0x9B0]) and is written as
camera := (point - 400, point - 300) by 0x5CA91A.
