## 6. Adjudication boundary
Both handlers CONSUME packets (0x4066C9 cursor readers) — they are inbound.
The payload shapes are mutually exclusive grammars over the same boat
object; the public-server send-side name BOAT_EFFECT=0x98 cannot be
transferred into the client recv namespace by name alone, and 0x97 has NO
name in either properties space. Per the T086.1 G17 discipline the two
values remain UNRESOLVED-VERSION-AUTHORITY at report level; this round
contributes the payload grammar, not a naming decision.
